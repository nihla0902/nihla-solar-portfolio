Shaded Residential Roof — Multi-Orientation Array
RESIDENTIAL · SHADED
2.35 kWSystem Size
₹16,753Annual Savings
6y 5mPayback Period
96%Consumption Offset

A small residential roof with a nearby palm tree casting partial shade. I split the array across four orientations (7 panels, 335 W each) to work around the obstruction, then modeled shading losses of 10.4% into the annual yield estimate.

Savings were calculated against Kerala State Electricity Board's telescopic domestic tariff, projecting ₹1.17 lakh net system cost against ₹4.74 lakh in lifetime bill savings over 25 years.

SPR-P3-335-BLK · 4 azimuths · KSEB LT-1 domestic telescopic tariff

A larger, unobstructed single-facing roof sized against a higher-usage load profile (~10,000 kWh/year). With no shading to route around, the array is a single 26-panel block generating more energy than the property consumes.

Modeled against KSEB's tiered commercial LT-VII(A) tariff — five consumption tiers from ₹6.05 to ₹9.40 per kWh — projecting ₹17.4 lakh in net savings over the system's lifetime.
